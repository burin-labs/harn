Repair queue.py, a cancellation-safe asyncio bounded queue. Preserve QueueClosed, AsyncQueue(capacity), async put(value), async get(), and close(). Capacity must be a positive integer excluding bool. A full queue blocks put until a slot is available; an empty open queue blocks get until an item is available. Items and blocked producers are FIFO. Cancelling a blocked put/get must remove its waiter without losing an item, consuming capacity, or leaving a later waiter stuck. close is synchronous and idempotent: reject pending and future puts with QueueClosed, drain already accepted items in FIFO order, and then reject pending/future gets with QueueClosed. A cancelled producer's value must never be delivered. Handle cancellation after a waiter is notified but before it resumes, and multiple waiting consumers on close. Use no elapsed-time assumptions. Inspect the implementation, repair it, and run verification.
