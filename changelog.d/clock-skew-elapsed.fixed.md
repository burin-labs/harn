Retention sweeps, host-lease wait accounting, and the scheduler no longer treat a start time ahead of "now" (clock skew, restored backup) as an enormous age: the new `harn_clock::elapsed_ms` clamps it to zero, so such sessions are not soft-/hard-deleted early and a lease wait keeps its full budget. `harn_clock::offset_datetime_to_ms` now floors pre-epoch instants.
