The process sandbox no longer switches off every Cargo `rustc` wrapper. It builds a scratch crate under the same
profile, keeps a wrapper that works there, and switches off one that cannot run or that leaves a confined
long-lived process behind. Each decision is logged as `process_sandbox_rustc_wrapper` with the wrapper, whether it
was kept or disabled, and why. A known compiler-cache wrapper has its server started outside the sandbox first.
Three wrapper cases join the sandbox conformance suite.
