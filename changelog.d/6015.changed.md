Routine Dependabot updates now arrive as one grouped PR per ecosystem. Each of
the Cargo, npm, and GitHub Actions ecosystems ends with a catch-all group
limited to `minor` and `patch`, so a week of ordinary bumps is one review and
rollback unit instead of a serial tail through the merge queue. Majors match no
group and still open one PR each, and the families that must bump together
(`cranelift`, `wasmtime`, `opentelemetry`, `react`/`react-dom`) keep their own
groups. `make check-dependabot-groups` now enforces that policy for every
ecosystem rather than only Cargo.
