Agent streams now emit typed generation, verification, and terminal phases.
Completion candidates remain provisional until the terminal phase supplies the
final visible reply and stop outcome, including during replay and ACP delivery.
Replay derives finality from the committed terminal record, so a partial save
cannot promote a provisional candidate.
The Rust `AgentEvent` enum includes a new `TurnPhaseChanged` variant.

Migration: handle `AgentEvent::TurnPhaseChanged` in exhaustive Rust matches.
Use `AgentTurnPhase::Verifying` for provisional candidates and
`AgentTurnPhase::Terminal` for the final reply and stop outcome.
