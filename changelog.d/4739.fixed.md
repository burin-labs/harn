- **Manifest hook handlers now resolve imported `pub fn`s that call their own siblings,
  regardless of which VM fires the hook (#4739).** A closure retained in a host registry
  (reminder providers, session/lifecycle hooks) previously pinned only its own module's
  scope. A handler that called an imported `pub fn` which in turn called a sibling in
  *that* module could throw `Undefined builtin: <sibling>` once the transiently-loaded
  module graph was torn down — most visibly when a lazily-resolved pre/post-tool hook
  fired from a short-lived child agent-loop VM. Retention now walks and pins the whole
  reachable graph of module scopes, so a hook body and everything it transitively imports
  keeps resolving after the loading VM is dropped.
